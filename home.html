Our Journey Through the Years

2019:🌸 Hamari Dosti Ka Pehla Saal 🌸

Is saal hamari dosti ne ek nayi pehchaan banayi hai. 💫 Humne ek dusre ko behtar samjha aur apni dosti ko ek mazboot neev di. 🤝 Tumhari meethi awaaz aur tumhari daya bhari baatein sabse khaas thi. 💖 Tumhara kindness ne mere dil mein ek khaas jagah banayi hai. 🌹 Dosti ka safar yahi tak hai, aur yeh safar hamesha yaad rahega! 😊

2020:💫 Hamari Dosti Ka Safar – Dusra Saal 💫

Is saal, tumhare efforts ne mujhe sabse zyada impress kiya hai. 🌟 Tumne mere saath baat karne ke liye email aur mere father ke WhatsApp ka bhi istemal kiya, aur yeh gesture mujhe bohot pasand aaya. 😊 Tumhara yeh effort mere liye sach mein special tha! Aur yeh bhi sach hai ki yeh wo daur tha jab mai tumhe aur tumhare calls ka intezaar karta tha. 📲 Tumhari call kuch hi minutes ki hoti thi, lekin wahi kuch minutes mera din banane ke liye kaafi hote the. 💖 Dosti ka yeh safar har din aur bhi yaadgar ho raha hai! 🌸

2021: 🌼 Hamari Dosti Ka Teesra Saal – Jazbaaton Ka Mod 🌼

Teesre saal mein hamari dosti aur gehri ho gayi... 🤍 Hum ek dusre ke dukh-sukh, har choti-badi baat, har expression aur har reply ko mehsoos karne lage the. 💬 Is dauraan, mujhe ehsaas hua ki main tumse pyar kar baitha hoon... ❤️ Maine iss saal tumhe impress karne ki koshish ki, apne jazbaat bayan kiye, aur tumse apne dil ki baat kehne ki himmat bhi juti... Lekin tumne apne wajah se politely mana kar diya, aur samjhaya ki mujhe abhi is sab mein nahi padna chahiye. 💭 Tumhari baaton mein samajh tha, aur tumhari soch mein maturity... 💫 Shayad isi wajah se meri izzat aur bhi badh gayi tumhare liye. Yeh saal ek naya mod tha, jahan jazbaat toh badle, lekin dosti wahi bani rahi – saaf, gehri aur sacchi. 🌿

2022: 🎉 Hamari Dosti Se Rishta Tak – Chautha Saal 🎉

Chautha saal... ek aisa daur jo kabhi bhool nahi sakta. 🌟 Ek lambi intezaar aur tumhe impress karne ki koshishon ke baad, April mein tumne finally 'haan' kaha... aur uss pal khushi ka toh thikana hi nahi tha! 🥹💖 Is saal hum pehli baar mile, saath waqt bitaya, hasin pal jeeye, aur apni yaadon mein kuch khubsurat lamhe hamesha ke liye basaa liye. ✨ Tumhara "yes" meri duniya ka sabse pyara lafz ban gaya... usi din se main sirf tumhara ho gaya. 🫶💕 Yeh saal sirf dosti ka nahi, ek nayi shuruaat ka tha — ek pyare rishte ki jo dosti se shuru hua tha. 💑🌈

2023: 💞 Rishton Ka Pehla Saal – Paanchva Saal 💞

Paanchva saal hamari relationship ka pehla aur sabse khaas saal raha... 🌸 Is saal humne sirf ek dusre ko samjha nahi, balki andar se mehsoos kiya... 💭 Har emotion, har secret, har baat – sab kuch humne khul kar baanta. 🗝️💬 Iss saal hum do baar mile, aur dono baar itna khoobsurat waqt saath bitaya ki lagaa jaise zindagi ruk gayi ho kuch pal ke liye... ⏳💑 Saath mein khana khaya, date pe gaye, ghoome phire, aur sabse khaas — tumhare haathon mein haath lekar lambi baatein ki. 🤝🌃 Yeh saal sirf relationship ka nahi, ek doosre ke dil mein basne ka tha. ✨💕

2024:🌟 Vishwas Aur Vaadon Ka Saal – Chhatha Saal 🌟

Chhatha saal... ek aur kadam kareeb aane ka. 💖 Iss saal hum 3 baar mile, aur pehli baar humne ek dusre ke parivaar ko samjha aur jaana. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Main tumhare kuch family members se mila, aur yeh connection aur bhi gehra ho gaya. 💫 Humne iss saal bhi saath mein bohot saari baatein ki, pal jeeye, aur khushiyon ko milkar mehsoosa. 🌈 Lekin haan... kuch fights bhi hui. 😔 Par unhi fights ke beech humne ek shapath li – “Ab kabhi ladai nahi, sirf pyaar aur samajh.” 🤝💞 Baby, maine vaada kiya hai ki main hamesha tumhare saath rahunga – har ek pal mein, har ek mod par. 🫶 Aur ab hum ek naye sapne ki tayaari kar rahe hain — ek behtar future, jisme sirf tum, main, aur humara saath ho... hamesha ke liye. 🌍💑💫
